Help & contact

HappyHome helps families set goals, challenges and rewards, so children can see what they're working towards and parents can cheer them on.

Get in touch

Email us. Please tell us what you were doing, and whether you use the website or the phone app. Never send your password.

Common questions

How does my child sign in?

On the sign-in screen, choose “Child sign in” and enter the family code and their name. The family code is in the parent's Settings, under Family settings. You can generate a new one any time.

How do I add another parent?

In Settings, choose “Create invite code”. The other parent creates an account, picks “Join with invite code” and enters it. Codes work once and last 7 days.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap “Forgot password?”. We'll email you a link to choose a new one. You can also change it when signed in: Settings, Account, help & legal, Change password.

My child forgot their family code

A parent can see it in Settings, under Family settings.

I'm not getting notifications

Open Settings and tap “Turn on notifications”, then allow them when asked. On a phone, also check that notifications are allowed for the app (or your browser) in the phone's own settings, and that battery saver isn't stopping it.

How does the photo check work?

If a parent asks for a photo as proof, the child takes one. An AI service looks at it and says whether it seems to show the task done. If “AI verification” is on for that goal, a clear match is approved automatically and parents can undo it. Otherwise it goes to the parent as usual.

How do I download my family's data?

Settings, Account, help & legal, “Download my family's data”.

How do I remove a child?

On the child's card in the Children section, choose Remove. This deletes their points, goals, challenges, notes and photos for good.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, Account, help & legal, “Delete my account”. You'll be asked for your password. If you are the account owner, this deletes the whole family and everything in it, so you may want to download a copy first. Any other parent only deletes their own login. It can't be undone.

Can children delete their own profile?

No. A parent removes a child's profile from their Children section.
