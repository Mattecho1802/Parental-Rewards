Weekly report

A summary of the week's goals, challenges, feedback and activities.

Reports are written by the app from the week's data. If an AI key has been added, the week's data goes to that AI service (Google Gemini or Anthropic) to write it in a friendlier voice. Children are sent as Child A and Child B, and the text of feedback notes is not sent. A new report is written every Sunday evening, and you get a notification when it is ready.
