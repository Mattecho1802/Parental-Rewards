Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

HappyHome is a family goals and rewards app. It is built for parents, and used by their children. This page explains what we keep, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain.

The short version
  • We keep only what the app needs to work.
  • No adverts, no tracking or analytics, and we never sell your information.
  • Parents control the family's data. You can download it or delete it all, inside the app.
  • Children do not create accounts or give us an email address. A parent sets up their profile.

Who we are

The service is run by the operator (“we”). For any privacy question, or to use your rights below, contact us.

What we collect

From the parent who creates the account

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed) by our sign-in provider, so nobody can read it, including us.
  • Your name and your family's name.

About children, entered by a parent

  • A name or nickname for each child. We suggest first names only or a nickname.
  • Their goals, challenges, rewards, points, activities, feedback notes and consequences, and what has been completed or approved.
  • Optional proof photos, if a parent has asked for a photo to show a task is done.

We do not ask for a child's email address, date of birth, address, school or location. Children sign in with the family code and their own name. Please do not put sensitive details in notes or goal names.

Technical information

  • Notification settings: if you turn notifications on, we keep the address your browser or phone gives us so we can send them. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Sign-in attempts are recorded briefly to slow down password guessing.
  • Settings kept on your own device only, such as your theme, widget layout and a sign-in token for children. These never leave your device.
  • Our hosting and database providers keep short-lived technical logs (for example, errors and request times) to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show goals and rewards, add up points, and let parents approve things.
  • To send notifications you asked for, such as reminders that something is due.
  • To provide optional AI features (see below).
  • To keep the service safe, fix problems, and answer your messages.

We do not use your family's information for advertising or to build profiles, and we do not sell or rent it.

AI features

Some features use an AI service, and only work if the service has been set up:

  • Photo checks. When a task needs a photo, the photo is sent to an AI service which says whether it looks like the task is done. A parent can choose to let a positive check approve a task automatically; parents are told and can undo it.
  • Weekly reports. To write the weekly summary, we send the week's activity to an AI service. Children are labelled “Child A”, “Child B” instead of by name, the family name is left out, and the text of feedback notes is not sent.

AI can make mistakes, so it never makes decisions that parents can't change.

Children

HappyHome is for parents to set up, and the parent gives permission for their child's use. You must be 18 or over to create an account. If you think a child has made an account or given us information without a parent knowing, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

  • Family information is kept until a parent deletes it or deletes the account.
  • Removing a child deletes their points, goals, challenges, notes, activities and photos.
  • Deleting the account owner's account deletes the whole family and everything in it, along with the other parents' logins. This happens straight away. Backups kept by our database provider can take a short time to roll off.
  • A proof photo is replaced if a task is done again, and removed when the task or child is deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download. In the app: Settings, Account, help & legal, Download my family's data.
  • Correct. You can edit names, goals and rewards in the app at any time.
  • Delete. In the app: Settings, Account, help & legal, Delete my account. A parent can also remove a single child.
  • Notifications. Turn them off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Other rights. Depending on where you live (for example, the UK or EU), you may also have the right to object to or restrict how we use your information, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Every family's information is separated so one family can't see another's, and proof photos are stored privately. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your family code private.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
